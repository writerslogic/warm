# Fact-store win search: pre-registration (written 2026-11-14 before any measurement)

Repo: writerslogic/warm main 14ca7ff3. Branch feat/fact-store-win. Harness: kill/kill_tests.py (numpy). Seeds: tuning seeds 0-1, report seeds 100-119 (20, paired: same keys, queries, projection for every arm). Real-data cells use 2 tuning + 10 report seeds. Cells run: B=2^14 with N<=3000, B=2^16 with all N, B=2^18 with N>=3000 (other cells are all-ties or below 1 bit/fact).

## Equivalence kills (no compute; recorded as outcome "tie by construction")
- bundle_bloom (OR of sparse indices) is a Bloom filter; counting bundle is a counting Bloom filter; dense bipolar bundle is a count-sketch/AMS projection; BlockCodeBundle is a partitioned counting Bloom filter.
- (i) merge by addition: Bloom OR, CMS add, IBLT add, HLL max already merge without raw facts. Equivalent, so tie.
- (iv) O(1) online update/delete: counting Bloom, cuckoo and quotient filters already do it, at 12-20 bits/item for 1% FPR; existing bundle_k_from_n_20261114.json shows the counting arm needs 32 bits/counter to match a plain Bloom. Dominated, no compute.
- (v) privacy: noised counting bundle is a Gaussian-noised count-min sketch (standard DP sketch). Equivalent, no compute.
- (vi-b) set similarity under noisy tokens by bundle-of-signatures cosine equals SimHash of the summed embedding (random projection is linear). Equivalent for the linear encoder; measured only where the per-item sign nonlinearity differs (K3).

## Measured candidates
All arms use the SAME sign-random-projection encoder of the key (dim d, Gaussian projection). The projection matrix is not counted for any arm. Bits count only the stored structure.

K1 near-key membership ("have I stored a fact like this?"). Members: stored key x_i queried with a noisy copy q (cosine rho to x_i). Non-members: fresh unrelated keys. Metric: AUC and TPR at 1% FPR. Keys: (a) synthetic Gaussian d=128; (b) real: LoCoMo locomo10.json "observation" sentences, hashed char-3gram TF-IDF (d=2048), queries = same sentence with 10/20/30% character substitutions; non-members = other sentences.
 VSA arm: counters w in {1,2,3,4,8} bits, D=B/w counters, bundle of sign codes, score = <S, sign code of q>. Static-quantised (best case) and streaming-saturating (online insert) both measured; the kill rule uses the weaker.
 Baselines at equal total bits B:
  B1 stored sign signatures, m=floor(B/N) bits each, score = max agreement over items (this is also "ANN over fact embeddings at equal bits" in its best 1-bit form; full-precision ANN holds only floor(B/(32d)) items, reported analytically as an upper bound on recall).
  B2 distance-sensitive Bloom filter: L bands of r projection bits hashed as tokens into a Bloom of B bits with k probes, score = fraction of the L tokens present; (r,L,k) grid-tuned per cell on tuning seeds (generous).
  B3 counting variant of B2 with 4-bit counters (B/4 cells) for the delete-capable comparison.
 Hyperparameters of every arm (w for VSA; m is forced for B1; r,L,k for B2) are tuned per cell on the tuning seeds and frozen for the report seeds.
 B1 also tries storing only a fraction f in {1,.5,.25,.125} of the items with longer signatures (graceful degradation). VSA static build quantises the sum to w bits with clip at kappa*sqrt(N), kappa in {1,2,3}. Sweep: B in {2^14, 2^16, 2^18} bits; N in {100,300,1000,3000,10000,30000}; rho in {0.95,0.9,0.8,0.7}. Bits/fact ranges 0.5 to 2600, so the cliff of each arm is located.
K2 near-key lookup (return a 10-bit value id for a noisy key). VSA: bundle of bind(sign code, value atom), cleanup over V=1024 atoms; baselines: stored signature (m bits) + 10-bit id with max-agreement scan; T-table LSH functions (idealised Bloomier at 1.23 x 10 bits per item per table, majority vote, best T tuned). Metric: top-1 value accuracy; same sweep reduced (B in {2^16,2^18}).
K3 set similarity of sessions: (a) exact-token sets, estimate Jaccard, metric RMSE, VSA count-sketch bundle (w bits, cosine to Jaccard) vs b-bit MinHash (b in 1,2,4,8; k=B/b) at B in {256,1024,4096}, J in {0.1,0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9}, n in {100,1000}; (b) noisy copies (facts perturbed rho=0.9): VSA bundle-of-signatures cosine vs MinHash over LSH-band tokens vs SimHash of mean embedding, metric: rank correlation with true set overlap.
K4 partial-cue role-filler retrieval: N records, R=6 roles, V=256 fillers/role; cue = random subset of 2..4 roles (and variant with 1 corrupted role); retrieve the missing filler. VSA: bundle of bound role-filler records + cleanup (existing semantics) vs exact table + (role,filler) inverted index (N*R*8 bits plus postings 2*log2(N) bits per entry). Equal bits and equal query time reported. Information bound: table cannot be beaten on exact retrieval at equal bits; measured to record the cliff.

## Kill rule (applies per candidate)
A candidate survives only if, over the 20 report seeds, the paired difference (VSA minus best baseline, each baseline at its tuned setting) in the task metric has a 95% bootstrap CI whose lower bound exceeds +0.01 AUC (K1), +0.02 accuracy (K2), a >10% lower RMSE (K3), or +0.02 accuracy (K4), in at least 3 cells (distinct (B,N) pairs) at 2 or more noise levels, on BOTH the synthetic and the real keys where both exist. A tie (CI contains 0) is "tie", not a win. The survivor must still win with the streaming-saturating counters if online insertion is claimed.
A hybrid counts only if the ablation without the superposed part loses.

# Round 2: pre-registration of Lead A and Lead B (written 2026-11-14 before any round-2 measurement)

Base: branch feat/fact-store-win at 81e8831. Harness: scripts/factstore_kill/round2/ (numpy; a standalone Rust program for the filter baseline). Results: benchmarks/results/factstore_kill_20261114/round2/. Seeds: tuning seeds 0-1, report seeds 100-119 (20, paired across arms) unless a cell below states fewer. Bootstrap: 4000 resamples over seeds. Governing rule for every cell is the round-1 rule (95% CI lower bound beyond a margin, at least 3 distinct cells at 2 or more levels). A second view with 99.9% intervals is reported as a multiplicity check (about 150 cells per lead, so about 4 false 95% wins are expected under the null); it does not replace the governing rule. Every cell is reported, including dead ones. Projection matrices, codebooks and hash seeds are not counted as bits for any arm. Timing runs are single threaded (OMP/OPENBLAS/MKL threads = 1), median of at least 50 queries after warm-up, on the GCP VM warm-factstore-1 (n2-standard-8).

## Lead A: many unknown roles, arbitrary query subsets

Task. N records with R roles, each filler uniform in V=256 values, independent. A query picks a stored record and a uniformly random u-subset U of roles (a different subset for every query, never declared in advance), supplies the other R-u fillers as the cue, and must return all u missing fillers. Success needs every filler exact. Metrics: accuracy per query; bits per record at matched accuracy (smallest grid point reaching 0.99 and, separately, 0.90); query time at matched bits. Both axes are reported for every cell whatever the verdict.
Cells: (R,u) in {(12,3),(12,4),(12,6),(16,3),(16,4),(16,8)}; N in {256,1024} with 20 report seeds and 100 queries per seed, N=4096 with 8 report seeds and 40 queries per seed for R=12 only (VSA build cost grows with N squared; this limit is part of the registration). Bits per record grid: 12,16,24,32,40,48,64,80,96,128,160,192; total bits B = N x bits per record. Exact content is 8R bits per record (96 at R=12, 128 at R=16), minus about log2 N for an unordered set.

Arms, all at equal total bits B:
- VSA-res: bundle of N bound records (role atom bound to filler atom, product over all R roles, summed), dense bipolar atoms, counters of w in {1,2,4} bits, D=B/w, static clip at kappa x sqrt(N) with kappa in {1,2} (best case; streaming counters are measured only if the arm survives). Decoding: multiply the bundle by the known role-filler products and the unknown role atoms, then resonator iteration over the u codebooks (V=256 atoms each), at most 40 iterations with early stop, rho restarts in {1,4} (the restart with the highest final tuple score wins). w, kappa, rho are tuned per cell on the tuning seeds and frozen. Timing includes all restarts.
- Resonator fairness check (decides whether the search or the store is failing): for every failed query compute the score of the true filler tuple and of the returned tuple. If the true tuple scores higher in more than 20% of failures in any cell where accuracy is below 0.99, the arm is re-run for that cell with 16 restarts and 100 iterations (the strong search) and the strong-search result is the VSA result.
- COL: exact column store, 8 bits per filler, scan with the known columns, fraction f=min(1, bits per record / 8R) of the records kept (graceful degradation; dropped records fail). Query time: vectorised scan.
- INV: one inverted index per role (posting lists of record ids per (role,filler), Elias-Fano cost measured from the actual lists, about (2+log2 V) R bits per record); known roles intersected smallest list first; missing fillers found by locating the record id in the V lists of each unknown role. Record fraction kept = min(1, bits per record / measured cost).
- ROWBF: one Bloom filter per record over the R tokens (role,filler), m = bits per record, k in 1..8 tuned. Candidates are the records whose filter contains all R-u known tokens; for each unknown role the answer is chosen uniformly among the fillers whose token is present (no present filler = failure).
- FPF: static fingerprint filter over full records (binary fuse), f-bit fingerprints, 1.13 f bits per record, any subset U answered by enumerating all V^u candidate tuples and testing each; the answer is uniform among the accepted tuples (the true tuple is always accepted). Accuracy is (1-exp(-lambda))/lambda with lambda=(V^u - 1) 2^-f; this model is validated against the real xorf BinaryFuse8/16/32 filter (bits per key, false-positive rate, enumeration time at u=3) on the VM, and the model fills the points between 8, 16 and 32. Time for u=4 is extrapolated from the measured per-probe cost at u=3 and labelled extrapolated; for u>=5 enumeration is infeasible (2^40 or more probes) and is reported as infeasible, not as a time.
- PAT (reference, analytic only): parity-address tables supporting arbitrary subsets need u+1 tables of u parity symbols at 1.23 x 8 bits, so (u+1) u 9.84 bits per record (118 at u=3, 197 at u=4). Accuracy is taken as 1 at or above that cost and 0 below (idealised static function, address collisions ignored, which favours PAT). Not run.
Baseline accuracy at a grid point is the maximum over the baselines at that point (each tuned). For the query-time comparison the best baseline is the most accurate one, and among baselines within 0.02 accuracy of it, the fastest.

Criterion A (applies to the VSA-res arm tuned on the tuning seeds).
- Bits win in a cell: at some grid point with VSA accuracy >= 0.90, the paired difference (VSA minus best baseline accuracy) has a 95% bootstrap CI lower bound above +0.02. The lead survives on bits only if bits wins occur in at least 3 distinct (R,u,N) cells, covering at least 2 distinct (R,u) pairs, all with R>=12 and u>=3.
- Overall survival (the only outcome that triggers engine implementation): a bits win and, in the winning cells, a VSA query time no more than 100 times the query time of the best baseline at those bits, measured under the timing protocol above. A bits win with a time ratio above 100 is reported as "bits-only win at the stated slowdown", is documented, and triggers no implementation. Anything else is a clean kill.
- Equality of bits at equal accuracy is a tie. Bits per record at matched accuracy is reported as the grid point, and linearly interpolated between grid points for display only.

## Lead B: real-text near-key membership

Task. Store N text facts; given a noisy query decide whether a stored fact is its source ("have I stored a fact like this?"). Positives: noisy copies of stored facts (1000 queries per seed; for N<1000 each fact is drawn repeatedly with an independent noise draw). Negatives: 4000 other facts per seed, none stored. Metrics: AUC, TPR at 1% FPR, TPR at 0.1% FPR (with 4000 negatives per seed the 0.1% threshold rests on 4 negatives per seed; the value reported is the mean over 20 seeds, which pools 80 such events, and this precision limit applies to every 0.1% figure).
Corpora (both with the same sentence rule). LoCoMo: locomo10.json observation sentences and dialog turns, whitespace normalised, length >= 30 characters, exact duplicates dropped (8,259 sentences, so N up to 3000 leaves 5,259 negatives). memory-lme-dev: the sessions of text-memory-lme-dev/input.json (the LongMemEval-S dev split used by the text benchmarks); user turns only (text after "user: " up to the next "assistant: "), split on sentence-ending punctuation, length 30 to 300 characters, duplicates dropped, a fixed random pool of 12,000 sentences (seed 12345).
Noise types: character substitution with rate 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 (round 1; substitutes from a 27-symbol alphabet) and word deletion with rate 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 (at least one word kept). Loads: N in {100,300,1000,3000}; B in {2^14, 2^16, 2^18} bits (skipped when B/N < 4). The three round-1 edge cells are re-run inside the grid and flagged: C1 = (LoCoMo, N=100, B=2^14, substitution 0.3), C2 = (LoCoMo, N=300, B=2^14, substitution 0.2), C3 = (LoCoMo, N=300, B=2^16, substitution 0.3); in round 1 all three used centred hashed char-3-gram TF-IDF keys (d=2048) with the strongest baseline near AUC 0.5.
Protocols. M (primary): negatives are corrupted with the same noise type and rate as the positives, so corruption itself carries no information. C (round 1): negatives are clean. Control K: positives are corrupted non-members and negatives are clean non-members; an arm with AUC above 0.5 on K detects corruption, not membership, and its protocol-C edge is not evidence of membership.

Arms, all at equal total bits B. Encoders E in {c3 = hashed char 3-grams, c5 = hashed char 5-grams, w12 = hashed word unigrams and bigrams}, d=2048, log TF x IDF, unit norm, centred on the corpus mean or not (tuned), one dense Gaussian projection per seed.
- VSA: bundle of sign codes, w in {1,2,3,4,8}, D=B/w, kappa in {1,2,3}, score = dot with the query's sign code; E and centring tuned. Streaming-saturating counters measured where VSA is the leading arm.
- sig (SimHash, stored signatures): m=floor(B/N) bits per fact, score = max agreement, f in {1,0.5,0.25,0.125} of facts kept; E and centring tuned. With E=c5 or w12 this is SimHash over those shingles.
- dsbf, dsbfc: distance-sensitive Bloom over projection bands (round 1), grid r in {9,12,16}, L in {24,64}, k in {1,3}, E=c3.
- SBF-global: one Bloom filter of B bits over the shingle tokens of all stored facts (shingle kind in c3, c5, w1, w2; k in {1,2,4,8}); score = fraction of the query's shingles present.
- SBF-rec: one Bloom filter per fact of m=B/N bits over its shingles (kinds c5, w2; k in {1,2,3}); score = max over facts of the fraction of query shingles present.
- CMS: global count-min sketch of shingle counts, D=B/w counters (w in {2,4,8}, saturating), rows in {1,2,4}, kinds c5 and w2; score = mean over the query's shingles of the minimum row counter. This is the conventional twin of the bundle (a linear detector over a sparse encoder).
- MH: b-bit MinHash, b in {1,2,4,8}, K=floor(B/(N b)) hashes per fact over shingles (kinds c5, w1, w2); score = max over facts of the b-bit-corrected agreement.
All hyperparameters are tuned per cell on the tuning seeds (objective: AUC under protocol M) and frozen. The strongest baseline per metric is chosen by the mean report-seed value of that metric (favourable to the baselines). Self-validation before the run: empirical Bloom false-positive rate against theory, MinHash estimate against exact Jaccard, and an in-code check that the shingle sets of a text and its copy agree.

Criterion B. A cell is a VSA win under protocol M when (i) the paired AUC difference against the baseline with the best mean AUC has a 95% CI lower bound above +0.01, and (ii) the paired TPR@1%FPR difference against the baseline with the best mean TPR@1% has a CI lower bound above 0 (a higher AUC with a lower TPR at 1% is not a win; the 0.1% figures are reported and not part of the rule). The lead survives only if wins occur in at least 3 distinct (N,B) cells at 2 or more noise levels on both corpora, and the VSA TPR@1% in the winning cells is at least 0.10 (otherwise the win is in the near-chance regime and is reported as inactionable). Wins in protocol C that do not appear in M are classed as corruption detection. The three flagged cells C1 to C3 are reported individually.
